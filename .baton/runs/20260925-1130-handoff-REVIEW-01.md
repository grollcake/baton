### Nits

- `internal/baton/remove.go` line 199 lists the record paths `remove` reports as
  kept (`BATON-LOG.txt`, `GUIDANCE.md`, `LESSON-LEARNED.md`, `lesson-learned`,
  `runs`, `CONCURRENCY.md`) and does not include `HANDOFF.md`. Nothing is lost
   -- the root scan deletes only managed documents and `VERSION`, so a sealed
  record survives `remove --apply` -- but it is silently absent from the kept
  list, where `CONCURRENCY.md`, its closest sibling, is named. One word.
- Sealing a record that is already superseded re-stamps `Log Line:` and makes it
  live again, with no warning and no test pinning the behaviour. That is
  defensible (re-sealing is an explicit act by a session that is stopping
  again), but it is currently undocumented in both `handoff.go` and
  `DIRECTOR.md`.
- `handoffState` clamps a negative `eventsSince` to `0`, so a record whose
  `Log Line:` is higher than the log's last line reads as live rather than
  unreadable. Only hand-editing the stamp or truncating the log can produce it,
  since the binary owns the stamp; worth a comment more than a code change.
